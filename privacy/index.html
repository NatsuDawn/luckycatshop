Lucky Cat Shop – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Lucky Cat Shop (“the game”) is made by NatsuDawn (“we”). This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

What the game keeps. Your progress (cat points, cats, items, settings, best score) is saved on your device. We do not run servers for the game, we do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, e-mail, contacts, photos or precise location. When you delete the game, its saves are removed from your device.

Advertising (mobile apps). The game offers optional rewarded videos, shown only when you tap a “watch ad” button. They are served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google’s SDK collects and shares with Google:

  • your device’s IP address (which can indicate your general location),
  • your device’s advertising ID and app set ID,
  • interactions with ads and the app (such as app launches, taps and video views),
  • diagnostic information about app and SDK performance.

This data is sent encrypted (TLS). Google’s use of it is described in Google’s privacy policy and at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices. In the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and US states with privacy laws, the game asks for your consent through Google’s consent form before personalised ads are shown; you can change your choice at any time from Settings > Privacy in the game. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalised ads when you choose to watch one.

Web versions. When you play on a web game portal (CrazyGames or Poki), the portal shows the ads and may store your progress in your browser or in your portal account. Their own privacy policies apply: crazygames.com/privacy-policy and poki.com/en/privacy.

Children. The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes. If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.

Contact. natsudawn65@gmail.com

《猫猫好运铺》隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 10 月 31 日

《猫猫好运铺》（下称“本游戏”）由 NatsuDawn（下称“我们”）制作。本政策说明本游戏使用哪些信息及原因。

本游戏保存的内容。你的进度（猫咪积分、猫咪、道具、设置、最高分）保存在你的设备上。我们没有为本游戏运营服务器，不要求注册账号，也不收集你的姓名、邮箱、通讯录、照片或精确位置。删除游戏即会清除设备上的存档。

广告（手机版）。本游戏提供可选的激励视频，只在你点击“看广告”按钮时播放，由 Google AdMob 提供。为展示和衡量广告、防止作弊，Google 的 SDK 会收集并发送给 Google：

  • 设备 IP 地址（可推断大致位置）；
  • 设备的广告 ID 和应用集 ID；
  • 与广告及应用的互动（如启动、点击、视频观看）；
  • 应用与 SDK 的性能诊断信息。

数据经加密（TLS）传输。Google 如何使用这些数据见 Google 隐私政策及 policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites。

你的选择。在欧洲经济区、英国、瑞士及设有隐私法的美国各州，本游戏会先通过 Google 的同意表单征求你的同意，再展示个性化广告；你可随时在游戏的“设置 > 隐私选项”中更改。你也可以在设备设置里重置或删除广告 ID，或关闭个性化广告。不同意时，你主动观看的广告仍可能是非个性化广告。

网页版。在网页游戏平台（CrazyGames 或 Poki）游玩时，由平台展示广告，进度可能保存在浏览器或平台账号中，适用平台自己的隐私政策：crazygames.com/privacy-policy、poki.com/en/privacy。

儿童。本游戏不面向 13 岁以下儿童，我们不会有意收集其个人信息。

变更。本政策如有变更，会在本页面发布新版本并更新日期。
